## LinkedIn

I'm teaching a new course at Ball State this spring: CS 439 Agentic Software Engineering.

Anyone can type a prompt. Leading a team of AI coding agents takes the engineering to make them deliver, and the judgment to know when they have.

Software engineering is changing. When each student works with a team of agents, they are the lead engineer: specifying, designing, reviewing, and deciding what ships. That role calls for software engineering expertise early, not years into a career.

In this course, each student leads their own team of AI coding agents and trains in the practices that role demands: requirements, design, testing, review, version control, CI/CD, and security.

Open to Ball State CS juniors and seniors (prerequisite: CS 222 Advanced Programming).
Tuesdays and Thursdays, 2:00–3:15 PM.

Course page: https://hergin.github.io/cs439
